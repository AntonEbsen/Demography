# 🚀 Launching: The "Iron & Altar" Research Monograph

I'm excited to share the culmination of my latest research into 19th-century Prussian demography.

My new digital monograph, **"The Iron and the Altar,"** explores how Bismarck’s Kulturkampf—a massive institutional assault on the Catholic Church—acted as a catalyst for the demographic transition in Prussia.

### 📊 Key Findings:
- Secularizing Catholic institutions (marriage, education, health) led to a significant structural decline in fertility.
- The effect was most pronounced in Polish-majority counties, revealing a fascinating intersection of nationalism and demography.
- This was not just a theological shift, but an institutional one.

### 🛠️ The Tech Stack:
- **Econometrics:** Two-Way Fixed Effects (TWFE) and Event Studies implemented in Python.
- **Reproducibility:** Fully modularized repo with automated CI/CD and Binder cloud-support.
- **Digital Monograph:** Built with Astro for a premium, high-performance reading experience.
